The ceiling for human achievement has been lifted

The decisive shift for business isn't AGI — it's agentic AI, and it's happening right now.

Agentic AI augments human capacity with better recall, faster data processing, and enhanced reasoning across the back office, front office, and corner office. Unlocking its value requires more than adopting new tools — it demands that leaders question old assumptions and drive the cultural change to thrive in this new era.

The 5 pillars of AI learning

A holistic strategy to build an AI-ready workforce — the ultimate driver of business value.

01 · Establish goalsexpand_more
Figure out what is most important and what you can actually measure. For example, a goal of 100% AI tool adoption — every team member using a Gemini Enterprise agent at some point in their workflow. Make sure the specific goal aligns to the bigger picture and is measurable.
02 · Secure sponsorshipexpand_more
Assemble three primary stakeholders: an executive sponsor (funding, high-level backing, consistent messaging), a groundswell lead (the "AI megaphone" running grassroots campaigns and collecting ideas), and an AI accelerator (the technical expert turning prioritized ideas into functional solutions).
03 · Sustain momentum and reward innovationexpand_more
Run the program as a "digital hub" featuring a gamified idea exchange with a leaderboard to continuously collect and reward AI use cases. Maintain momentum with regular, multichannel communication and a quarterly awards program to recognize top innovators.
04 · Integrate AI into daily workflowsexpand_more
Keep the education going with internal hackathons where small teams develop and pitch innovative AI solutions, and challenge-based "Field Days" — in-person and virtual — where teams practice using the new custom AI tools in a collaborative setting.
05 · Prepare for increasing risks with trusted frameworksexpand_more
As agent-accelerated cyber risks grow more sophisticated, security becomes everyone's responsibility. Train employees on what data can (and cannot) be used in AI tools, and how to recognize sophisticated threats like AI-driven social engineering.
